TRUST BY DESIGN

Healthcare automation needs controls, not hype.

Human review

Clinical decisions and sensitive submissions stay under authorized human control.

Minimum necessary access

Design workflows around the least data needed for the administrative task.

Auditability

Preserve a clear history of what the system suggested, what staff changed and what was submitted.

Compliance roadmap

Security, vendor agreements, access controls and HIPAA obligations must be validated before production handling of protected health information.
